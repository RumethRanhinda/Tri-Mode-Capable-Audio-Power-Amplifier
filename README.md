# Tri-Mode Capable Audio Power Amplifier

An audio power amplifier designed to operate in three distinct configurations: **Stereo**, **Mono**, and **Bridge Mode**. This project explores advanced analog circuit design, focusing on power efficiency, signal integrity, and flexible output configurations. 

Developed as part of the **EN2111** module at the Department of Electronic and Telecommunication Engineering (ENTC), University of Moratuwa.

## 🚀 Operating Modes

*   **Stereo Mode:** Drives two independent channels (Left and Right) for standard dual-speaker setups.
*   **Mono Mode:** Outputs a single mixed audio channel, ideal for public address systems or single-speaker applications.
*   **Bridge-Tied Load Mode:** Cascades two amplifier channels to drive a single load. This configuration effectively quadruples the theoretical power output across the speaker without requiring a higher power supply voltage.
